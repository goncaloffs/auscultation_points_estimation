# Auscultation-Points-Estimation

Auscultation, a crucial practice in medicine, plays a fundamental role in detecting cardiac and pulmonary signals, contributing to accurate diagnoses. 

<img width="272" height="112" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1deff3db-774b-4f73-9e08-712993299140" />















However, the lack of organic teaching of auscultation and its inadequate practice have had a negative impact on the clinical competence of physicians in training, also reflecting a diminished academic interest. 
Consequently, auscultation has entered a phase of decline. In order to overcome this problem, this work proposed the estimation of cardiac and pulmonary auscultation points with the aid of MediaPipe. A Python application was developed that allows the estimation of auscultation points in three different modes: anterior and posterior pulmonary auscultation, and cardiac auscultation. 
During the technical analysis, the developed program revealed promising results, standing out for its high precision in estimating auscultation points in all modes. However, it showed some limitations in the correct identification of points in situations where the distance from the patient to the system is very large or very small.
